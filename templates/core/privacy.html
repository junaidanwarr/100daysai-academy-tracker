Privacy policy

The 100DaysAI Academy Tracker ("the tracker") is the system 100DaysAI Academy uses to follow each student from enrolment and niche research to a live YouTube channel. This page explains what it stores, why, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

What we store

  • Enrolment details the academy enters: your name, guardian, email, phone, city, country, batch, instructor and dates.
  • Your work: niche research, channel research sheets, assignment hand-ins, and the scores and feedback you receive.
  • Your channel record: the channel name, link and ID you submit, its niche and upload plan.
  • Public YouTube figures for that channel: subscribers, views, video count and each video's views, likes and comments. Anyone can see these on YouTube.
  • YouTube Analytics, only if you choose to connect it: watch time, impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, audience retention and traffic sources. Revenue is read only if the academy has turned that on and you approve it.
  • Account and security records: your sign-in email, sign-in times, and an audit log of changes. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.

YouTube and Google data

The tracker uses YouTube API Services. By connecting your channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

When you click Connect analytics and approve it at Google, the tracker receives read-only access:

  • youtube.readonly: to confirm the Google account owns the channel and to read its videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the channel's analytics listed above.
  • openid and email: to record which Google account gave consent.

The tracker cannot upload, edit or delete videos, change channel settings, post comments, or read your email, Drive or any other Google data. The access token is stored encrypted and used only to fetch these figures.

The tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to show you and your instructors your channel's progress. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared outside the academy.

Why we use it

To run the course: track research and assignments, confirm channels, show your progress, and alert your instructor when you may need help (for example a missed deadline or a channel with no uploads).

Who can see it

  • You, in your own portal.
  • Your assigned instructor and the academy's administrators.
  • Academy management, read-only.

Other students cannot see your records. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

Service providers process data on the academy's behalf only to run the tracker: the hosting provider, Google (YouTube APIs), and, where enabled, an email delivery service for alerts.

Withdrawing YouTube access

You can stop the tracker reading your analytics at any time, either way:

  • In the tracker: My channels → your channel → Disconnect.
  • At Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove the 100DaysAI Academy Tracker.

Disconnecting stops new analytics straight away and deletes the stored access token. Figures already collected stay as part of your course record unless you ask for them to be deleted.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are enrolled and for the academy's record of your batch afterwards. YouTube data obtained through the API is refreshed regularly and deleted when it is no longer needed for the course, or on request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, including all YouTube data the tracker holds about your channel. {% if contact_email %}Email {{ contact_email }}.{% else %}Contact your academy administrator.{% endif %} We reply within 30 days.

Cookies

The tracker uses only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in and protect forms. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. A research sheet you load without submitting stays in your own browser and is not sent to the academy.

Changes
