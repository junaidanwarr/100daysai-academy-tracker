Why some columns are empty

The distinction this system refuses to blur.

Subscribers, views, video counts and upload dates come from the public YouTube Data API and need only a channel ID. These metrics — {{ private_metrics|join:", " }} — belong to the channel owner and are readable only after that student grants access to their own YouTube Analytics.

An em dash means the figure could not be obtained. A zero means it was measured and is zero. The two are stored differently and are never conflated.
